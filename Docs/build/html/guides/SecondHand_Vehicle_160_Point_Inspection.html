160-Point Vehicle Inspection Checklist (Second-Hand Vehicles)

1. Road Test

  1. Cold engine operation performs properly

  2. Throttle operates properly during cold start

  3. Vehicle operates properly during warm-up conditions

  4. Engine performs properly at operating temperature

  5. Engine reaches normal operating temperature

  6. No abnormal vibrations or noises during brake applications

  7. Engine fan(s) operate properly

  8. Brake pedal free play and travel within specifications

  9. ABS operates properly (via diagnostic check)

  10. Parking brake operates properly and within specification

  11. Seat belt material condition

  12. Seat belts operate properly with smooth extension/retraction

  13. Transmission/clutch operates smoothly

  14. Automatic transmission functions at cold temperature

  15. Automatic transmission functions at operating temperature

  16. Steering feels normal during lock-to-lock turning

  17. Steering wheel centered in straight driving

  18. Vehicle tracks straight on level road

  19. Tilt & telescopic steering operates properly

  20. Windshield washer fluid sprays properly

  21. Front windshield wiper operates properly in all modes

  22. Rear windshield wiper operates properly in all modes

  23. Wipers return to rest position correctly

  24. Replace wiper blades/inserts if needed

  25. Speedometer functions properly

  26. Odometer registers mileage correctly

  27. Cruise control operates properly

  28. Heater operates properly

  29. A/C operates properly

  30. No abnormal engine noise during driving

  31. No interior squeaks/rattles

  32. No excessive wind or road noise

  33. No tire vibration/steering wheel shimmy during road test

2. Frame, Structure & Underbody

  1. Frame/Unibody condition

  2. Panel alignment and fit

  3. Lower body and underbody condition

  4. Suspension items free from leaks & wear

  5. Inspect struts/shocks for leaks/wear

  6. Power steering system free from leaks

  7. Wheel covers secure and undamaged

  8. Tires inflated, free of damage/defects

  9. Minimum 5/32” tread depth across width

  10. All tires same brand, size, rating per OEM specs

  11. Brake calipers & lines free from damage/leaks

  12. Brake system fully equipped (shims, pins, clips)

  13. Brake pads/shoes have min. 50% life remaining

  14. Brake rotors/drums within spec, no abnormal wear

  15. Exhaust system secure & free of leaks

  16. Engine/transmission mounts in good condition

  17. Drive/axle shafts operate properly, no damage

  18. CV joints/boots free from wear/leaks

  19. Engine free from fluid leaks

  20. Transmission case/pan free from leaks

  21. Differential fluid correct, no leaks

3. Under Hood

  1. Drive belts and hoses free from cracks/damage

  2. Confirm under-hood labels and decals present

  3. Air filter in good condition

  4. Battery free of visible damage

  5. Battery test performed (Midtronics)

  6. Battery posts & cables free from corrosion/damage

  7. Battery properly sized & secured

  8. Charging system working within spec

  9. Engine coolant level correct

  10. Coolant protection level (-31°F)

  11. Change engine oil and filter

  12. Check for oil sludge/gel evidence

  13. Check all fluid levels (brake, clutch, power steering, transmission, washer, coolant)

  14. Fluids clear & uncontaminated

4. Functional & Walkaround

  1. Owner’s Manual and warranty booklet present

  2. Fuel gauge operational

  3. Battery voltage gauge operational

  4. Engine temperature gauge operational

  5. Horn operates properly

  6. Airbag(s) present, no deployment signs

  7. Headlights aligned & functioning (low/high)

  8. Emissions test performed if required

  9. Tail lights function correctly

  10. Brake lights function correctly

  11. Side marker lights function correctly

  12. Backup lights function correctly

  13. License plate lights function correctly

  14. Exterior lights free of cracks/haze/damage

5. Interior Functions

  1. Instrument panel/warning lights function properly

  2. HVAC panel/lights operate properly

  3. Instrument light dimmer functions correctly

  4. Turn signals operate & self-cancel

  5. Hazard flashers operate properly

  6. Rear-view interior mirror intact & adjustable

  7. Exterior mirrors adjust & in good condition

  8. Remote mirror control functions correctly

  9. Glass free from cracks/large chips

  10. Window tint compliant with laws

  11. Dome/courtesy lights operate properly

  12. Power windows operate from all switches

  13. Window locks function properly

  14. Audio/CD/Aux system operational

  15. Audio speakers function without distortion

  16. Antenna present & functional

  17. Clock operates correctly

  18. 12v power outlet operational

  19. Ashtrays (if equipped) intact & clean

  20. Headliner, sun visors, trim panels in place & good condition

  21. Floor mats OEM, properly secured

  22. Doors open/close properly

  23. Door locks incl. child locks function properly

  24. Remote keyless entry works properly

  25. 2 Master keys present

  26. Theft deterrent system functions correctly

  27. Seat adjustments work properly

  28. Seat heaters (if equipped) function properly

  29. Memory seat control operates properly

  30. Headrests function properly

  31. Rear defogger works properly

  32. Defogger indicator light works

  33. Luggage compartment light functions

  34. Luggage compartment clean, free of debris

  35. Hood and trunk latches operate properly

  36. Emergency trunk release functional

  37. Fuel door release functions properly

  38. Spare tire cover present and in good condition

  39. Spare tire or inflator kit present

  40. Spare tire tread depth >5/32”

  41. Spare tire inflated to correct pressure

  42. Spare tire undamaged

  43. Spare tire secured properly

  44. Jack and tools present & secured

  45. Acceptable aftermarket items only

  46. Removal of unacceptable aftermarket items

6. Exterior Appearance

  1. Body surface condition good

  2. Exterior washed & clean

  3. Paint free of swirl marks, high luster finish

  4. Paint free of scratches/chips (reasonable)

  5. Wheels/wheel covers clean

  6. Wheel wells clean

  7. Tires clean & dressed

  8. Engine compartment clean

  9. Under-hood insulation pad clean

  10. Engine & compartment dressed properly

  11. Door jambs clean

  12. Glove box & console compartments clean

  13. Cabin air filter condition checked

  14. Seats, carpets, mats free of stains

  15. Vehicle free from odors/heavy fragrances

  16. Glass clean & streak-free

  17. Interior free of debris

  18. Dash & vents clean/dressed

  19. All crevices clean

  20. Upholstery and panels in good condition

  21. Vehicle free of improper paint repairs

  22. Glass free of improper repairs

  23. Bumpers free from cuts/gouges

  24. Interior surfaces (leather/vinyl/plastic) free from excessive wear

7. Optional/Additional Systems

  1. Sunroof/convertible top operates fully

  2. Seat heaters function correctly

  3. Navigation system works properly, memory cleared

  4. Head unit software updated

  5. Transfer case (4WD) switches smoothly

  6. Truck bed condition good

  7. Truck bed liner secure & intact

  8. Backup camera functions properly

8. Advanced Safety Systems (if equipped)

  • SOS (Safety Connect) indicator green

  • Lane Keep Assist operates properly

  • Adaptive Cruise/Pre-Collision Systems work properly

  • Intelligent Parking Assist functions correctly

9. Hybrid Components (if applicable)

  • Hybrid battery condition

  • Battery control module operational

  • Hybrid power management system functional

  • Electric motor/generator functions correctly

  • ECVT operates smoothly

  • Power inverter functions correctly

  • Inverter coolant level correct

  • EV/Eco/Power modes function correctly

  • Hybrid transaxle “park” mechanism functional

  • Multi-information display operates correctly

  • Touch Tracer display operates correctly

  • Hill Start Assist functions correctly

  • Remote air conditioning works (if equipped)

  • Solar ventilation system works (if equipped)
