Quarterly Vehicle Health Inspection Checklist (50-Point)

1. Engine & Powertrain

  1. Check engine oil level & quality

  2. Inspect oil filter condition

  3. Check coolant level & leaks

  4. Inspect drive belts for cracks/wear

  5. Inspect hoses for leaks or soft spots

  6. Inspect air filter condition

  7. Inspect cabin air filter condition

  8. Check transmission fluid level & leaks

  9. Inspect engine/transmission mounts

  10. Check for fluid leaks under engine & gearbox

2. Electrical & Battery

  1. Test battery voltage & charging system

  2. Inspect battery terminals for corrosion

  3. Test alternator output

  4. Test starter motor performance

  5. Check all fuses and relays

3. Brakes & Suspension

  1. Inspect brake pads/shoes (thickness)

  2. Inspect brake discs/drums (damage/warping)

  3. Check brake fluid level & condition

  4. Test parking brake function

  5. Inspect shocks/struts for leaks

  6. Inspect suspension bushings & joints

  7. Check wheel bearings for noise/play

4. Steering & Tires

  1. Test steering response & play

  2. Check steering fluid level & leaks

  3. Measure tire tread depth (>5/32”)

  4. Check tire pressure (all tires + spare)

  5. Inspect tire wear patterns

  6. Inspect wheels & rims for damage

5. Exhaust & Emissions

  1. Inspect exhaust for leaks/damage

  2. Check catalytic converter/muffler condition

  3. Confirm no exhaust warning lights

6. Safety Equipment

  1. Test seat belts (operation & condition)

  2. Check airbags (warning light off)

  3. Test horn function

  4. Inspect first-aid kit contents

  5. Confirm warning triangle/reflective vest present

7. Lighting & Visibility

  1. Test headlights (low/high beam)

  2. Test brake/reverse/fog lights

  3. Test turn signals & hazard lights

  4. Test interior dome/courtesy lights

  5. Check windshield for cracks/chips

  6. Test wiper blades & washer spray

  7. Test rear defogger/heater operation

  8. Inspect mirrors (adjustment & condition)

8. HVAC & Interior

  1. Test air conditioning & heating performance

  2. Check ventilation airflow

  3. Inspect seat adjustments & seat heaters

  4. Test power windows & locks

9. Technology & Driver Assist

  1. Test infotainment system & Bluetooth/USB

  2. Test rear-view camera/parking sensors
